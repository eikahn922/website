Turning Lived Experience into Advocacy

Born with Pierre Robin sequence, Ezra brings firsthand experience with craniofacial care to his work as a myFace spokesperson and fundraiser. He uses his story to support people with craniofacial differences and help build understanding around the medical, social, and personal impact of craniofacial conditions.

  • Advocates publicly for people and families affected by craniofacial differences.
  • Supports fundraising and awareness efforts for myFace's craniofacial community.
  • Received the 2023 Ramey-Ross Award for exceptional contribution and dedication to the cleft and craniofacial community.
