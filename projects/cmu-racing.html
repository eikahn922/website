Structures Subteam

Carnegie Mellon Racing builds an electric Formula SAE racecar. I joined the structures subteam in October 2024 on battery-enclosure work, and have been with the suspension group since May 2026.

Suspension Welding Jig

I designed a welding jig for the suspension tubes. Each member is located on a common plate by its own pair of blocks, which fix the tube at its designed angle and length before anything is tacked.

The point of a jig here is repeatability. Welding puts a lot of heat into thin-wall tube, and a joint tacked freehand pulls as it cools, so the geometry you get is not the geometry you drew. Fixturing every member against hard locations means each assembly comes off the plate the same way, and the left and right sides match.

Battery Enclosure

From October 2024 to May 2026 I modelled battery-enclosure components in SolidWorks, working the structural, packaging, and electrical-integration requirements into the component geometry, and coordinated with the structures and electrical-integration groups to keep enclosure geometry, component placement, and the mechanical interfaces aligned across the assembly.
