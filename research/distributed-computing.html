Electronics Packaging

I designed and fabricated the hardware enclosure for a distributed exoskeleton communications node, keeping the electronics close to the sensor to reduce long cable runs.

Packaging requirements

  • Lightweight and quick to 3D print.
  • Shield status LEDs from infrared motion-capture cameras while preserving debug access.
  • Keep plugs accessible and transfer cable loads into the housing—not the board connectors.
  • Constrain the Teensy, IMU, and CAN Pal without loading surface-mounted components.

Why it changed

I replaced the larger Teensy/CAN arrangement with exactly one QT Py and one IMU. The smaller controller reduced enclosure volume while retaining connector access, strain relief, LED shielding, printability, and serviceability.
